About
NEO is the undergraduate journal of philosophy of New York University. Founded in 2023 and operated by current NYU students, NEO publishes cutting-edge philosophical papers by undergraduates around the globe every Spring. By undergraduate, for undergraduate!

NEO is affiliated with the Department of Philosophy at NYU, widely considered one of the most prestigious in the world and home to central figures in contemporary analytic philosophy like Thomas Nagel and David Chalmers. Starting 2027, every issue of NEO will include a short commentary by a current NYU faculty alongside student publications.

NEO has extensive cooperation with the philosophy forum club at NYU, which regularly hosts a wide array of events. We welcome anyone interested to join us at the 6th floor lounge at 5 Washington Place for philosophical discussions.
NEO is generously supported by the NYU College of Arts and Science.
