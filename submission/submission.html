Submission Guidelines
Aims and Scope
NEO is an undergraduate journal of philosophy publishing original work from students worldwide. In line with the vision of its host institution, it aims to explore the frontiers of philosophical research and render philosophy accessible to a wider audience.
  • We welcome submissions in all areas and traditions of philosophy.
  • Interdisciplinary work is welcome when it makes a substantive philosophical contribution.
  • Submissions are evaluated without preference for any particular philosophical school, method, or tradition.
Eligibility
  • Authors must be current undergraduate students or recent graduates who obtained an undergraduate degree within one year.
  • Authors need not be philosophy majors or minors.
  • Coauthored submissions are permitted where all authors satisfy the eligibility requirements, and all consent to publication in Neo.
  • Members of the editorial board may not submit during their term of service.
Manuscript
  • Papers should aim for 1500-4000 words.
  • Papers should include a 50-200 word abstract.
  • Manuscripts up to 5000 words may be considered where additional length is warranted.
  • The word count includes notes but excludes the abstract and bibliography.
Submissions should:
  • advance or critically examine a philosophical thesis or argument;
  • demonstrate clarity, argumentative rigor, and intellectual independence;
  • engage relevant literature where appropriate; and
  • be accessible to a general philosophical readership.
Purely expository papers will ordinarily not be considered unless they make a substantial interpretive or philosophical contribution.
Manuscript Preparation
Submissions should include:
  • a title;
  • an abstract of approximately 50–200 words;
  • 3–6 keywords; and
  • a complete bibliography.
Manuscripts should:
  • be submitted in .doc, .docx, or PDF format;
  • use a readable 11- or 12-point font and standard margins;
  • include page numbers; and
  • contain no identifying information.
NEO’s accepted styles of citation include APA, Chicago author-date, and Chicago notes-and-bibliography at initial submission. Chicago author-date is preferred and will be used for final publication.
Double-Blind Peer Review
NEO uses double-blind peer review.
  • Authors’ identities are concealed from reviewers.
  • Reviewers’ identities are concealed from authors.
  • Manuscripts should not contain the author’s name, institution, acknowledgments, or other identifying information.
  • Authors should remove identifying information from the document where practicable, including from the content of the paper when referencing their own work.
Submissions will first undergo editorial screening for scope, eligibility, and basic scholarly standards. Manuscripts selected for review will be evaluated by at least two readers. Editorial decisions will be made before March 2027.
Simultaneous and Prior Submissions
  • Simultaneous submissions are permitted.
  • Authors should indicate whether they are simultaneously submitting to other journals, and email us if they later decide to submit to other journals.
  • Having simultaneous submissions does not affect the chance of one’s article being accepted.
  • Authors must notify the editors promptly and withdraw the manuscript if it is accepted for publication elsewhere.
  • Manuscripts must not have been previously published in substantially the same form.
  • Coursework, conference presentations, and undergraduate theses deposited in institutional repositories do not ordinarily count as prior publication.
Academic Integrity and AI
Authors are responsible for the originality and accuracy of their work.
  • Plagiarism, fabricated quotations or citations, and other forms of academic misconduct are grounds for rejection or retraction.
  • The substantive philosophical argument and analysis must be the work of the human author or authors.
  • Use of generative AI in developing the argument, research, structure, bibliography, or text must be disclosed upon submission.
  • Authors remain responsible for verifying all claims, quotations, and references.
Conflicts of Interest
  • Authors should disclose any relationship that could reasonably create a conflict with an editor..
  • Editors and reviewers must recuse themselves where personal, academic, supervisory, or other relationships could compromise impartial evaluation.
Copyright and Open Access
  • Authors retain copyright in their work.
  • By accepting publication, authors grant NEO the right of first publication and a continuing nonexclusive right to archive and distribute the article.
  • Published work will be freely available online under CC BY.
  • No submission or publication fees are charged.
Submission
Submit manuscripts through this Google form by March 30th. The submission form will request:
  • author name and contact information;
  • institutional affiliation;
  • confirmation of eligibility and originality;
  • disclosure of relevant conflicts of interest; and
  • disclosure of substantive generative-AI use, if applicable.
Identifying information should appear only in the submission form, not in the manuscript.
